# Pricing plan (draft 3)

## Subscription

Two 250 g bags a month for 18 EUR, billed monthly. Customers can pause for up to two months.

## One-off bags

- House blend, 250 g: 9.50 EUR
- Single origin, 250 g: 12 EUR

## Margin check

At 6.85 EUR per kilo for green coffee, the house blend keeps a margin above 40 percent. The single
origin needs the natural lot at 7.40 EUR or less to stay above 35 percent.
