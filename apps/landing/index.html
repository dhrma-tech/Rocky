Questions.

What does Rocky store, and where?

Documents, transcripts, extracted commitments and the search index, in one SQLite file in your data folder (by default %APPDATA%\Rocky on Windows). Original files and recordings sit next to it. Nothing is stored on a server.

How do I delete my data?

Remove a single source, a connector with everything it synced, or use Delete everything in Settings. Deletion also removes derived chunks, summaries and flashcards.

Which models does it support?

Local models through Ollama, and Claude with your Anthropic key. Gemini and OpenAI-compatible endpoints can be configured. Model choices live in a config file you can edit.

Does it work offline?

Yes, in local-only mode with Ollama installed. Search, transcription and answers then run on your machine, and every outside model call is blocked. Answers from small local models are slower and less precise.

What can connectors access?

Each connector asks for the narrowest scopes it needs and starts read-only. Writes, such as a Gmail draft or a Linear issue, are separate permissions you turn on, and each one still waits for your approval.

How do approvals work?

Rocky proposes an action with its exact payload. You approve that payload, and the approval is bound to its hash, so an edited payload needs a new approval. Every step is written to an append-only audit log. Email is never sent, only drafted.

What does it cost?

Rocky is free. With local models there is no cost beyond your hardware. With an API key you pay your provider directly, and Rocky enforces a monthly budget cap ($10 by default).

What is the license?

Apache-2.0. You can use, change and redistribute it, including commercially.

Is recording meetings allowed?

Laws differ by place. Rocky asks you to confirm that participants know and that recording is lawful before every recording, and logs that confirmation. It is a reminder, not legal advice.

How is this different from a cloud assistant?

Your index stays on your machine, every answer cites its source or says it found nothing, and nothing is written to your apps without your approval. It is early software: expect rough edges.
